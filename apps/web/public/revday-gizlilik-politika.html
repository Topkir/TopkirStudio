# RevDay Privacy Policy Last updated: September 15, 2026 At RevDay ("the app", "we"), we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect while you use the app and how we use it. ## Data We Collect * Account info: your email address (used for email sign-in) * Onboarding answers: skill level, budget range, weekly availability, work-style preferences * Progress data: completed days, streak count, watched lessons * Earnings logs: income amounts you choose to log inside the app * AI coach chat history: messages you exchange with the coach, processed server-side to generate responses ## How We Use Your Data We use your data only to personalize your experience - matching you to the right income path, showing your progress, and running the AI coach. We do not sell your data to third parties. ## Data Storage Your data is stored in a database protected by row-level security policies - only you can access your own data. ## Your Subscription Payments and subscriptions are handled through the App Store / Google Play and our subscription management provider; we never see your card details. ## Your Rights If you would like your account and data deleted, contact us at: ahmettopkir@topkirstudio.com ## Changes This policy may be updated from time to time; you will be notified in-app of any material changes.
